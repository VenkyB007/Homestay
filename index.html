Why choose Gotul Hampi

A perfect place for families to stay, relax, and explore Hampi.

Gotul Hampi is a comfortable two-bedroom homestay for families and small groups who want a peaceful and private stay. The whole upstairs is yours, with a terrace to unwind on and the ruins of Hampi ten minutes down the road.

Around Hampi

Nearby, by road from our gate.

Road distances from the house. Ask us for a driver or a cycle route.

Virupaksha Temple

Vittala Temple and the stone chariot

Tungabhadra Dam and gardens

Lit up in the evenings on weekends.

Anegundi and the coracle crossing

Quieter than Hampi, same boulders.

Daroji Sloth Bear Sanctuary

Bears come out around 4 pm.

Sanapur Lake

Swim, or watch others jump off the rocks.
